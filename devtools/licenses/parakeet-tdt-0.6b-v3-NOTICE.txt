Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3 (multilingual) — ONNX, int8
=================================================

This archive contains the NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3 speech recognition model,
packaged for import into the Parakeetype Android keyboard.

Original work
  "parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3" by NVIDIA
  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3

Changes made to the original work
  - Exported from NeMo to ONNX (encoder, decoder/joint network and the
    nemo128 preprocessor) and quantised to int8; vocabulary extracted to
    vocab.txt. Conversion by istupakov:
    https://huggingface.co/istupakov/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-onnx
  - Repackaged, without further modification, into this single ZIP archive
    by the Parakeetype project.
