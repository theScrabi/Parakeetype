Parakeet Ultra (multilingual) — ONNX, int8
==========================================

This archive contains the Parakeet Ultra speech recognition model, packaged for
import into the Parakeetype Android keyboard.

Original work
  "parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3" by NVIDIA
  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
  further trained as "parakeet-ultra" by Moondream
  https://huggingface.co/moondream/parakeet-ultra

Changes made to the original work
  - Exported to ONNX (encoder, decoder, joiner) and quantised to int8;
    vocabulary extracted to tokens.txt. Conversion by mldecode:
    https://huggingface.co/mldecode/parakeet-ultra-onnx-int8
  - The nemo128 preprocessor is taken from the Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3 ONNX export
    by istupakov (https://huggingface.co/istupakov/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-onnx).
  - Repackaged by the Parakeetype project into this single ZIP archive, with
    tokens.txt renamed to vocab.txt and no further modification.
