Parakeetype is a free and open-source speech-to-text keyboard for Android.

All transcription happens entirely on your device using the NVIDIA Parakeet ASR model via ONNX Runtime - no audio ever leaves your phone, no account required, and the app has no internet access at all.

<b>Features</b>

• Fully on-device inference - completely private
• Works as a standard Android Input Method (IME) in any app
• No internet permission: download the model file once in your browser and import it
• No Google Play Services, Firebase, or any third-party telemetry
• Minimal permissions: microphone, notifications, foreground service

<b>Requirements</b>

• Android 12 (API 31) or higher
• ~700 MB free storage for the model file

<b>Open Source</b>
